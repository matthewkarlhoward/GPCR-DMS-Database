**library construction protocol

In the first round of PCR (18 cycles), the CCR5 and CXCR4 coding sequences were amplified as three overlapping fragments of ~800 bp each.
Primer pair: CCR5 fragment A: MiSeq_CCR5_myctag_F (TCTTTCCCTACACGACGCTCTTCCGATCTCGAGCAAAAGCTGATCTCAG) and MiSeq_CCR5_652_R (GTGACTGGAGTTCAGACGTGTGCTCTTCCGATCTTAGGATTCCCGAGTAGCAG)
Primer pair: CCR5 fragment B: MiSeq_CCR5_176_F (TCTTTCCCTACACGACGCTCTTCCGATCTAAGGCTGAAGAGCATGACTG) and MiSeq_CCR5_875_R (GTGACTGGAGTTCAGACGTGTGCTCTTCCGATCTTGATGCAGCAGTGCGTCAT)
Primer pair: CCR5 fragment C: MiSeq_CCR5_388_F (TCTTTCCCTACACGACGCTCTTCCGATCTCGTCCATGCTGTGTTTGCT) and MiSeq_Venus_468_R (GTGACTGGAGTTCAGACGTGTGCTCTTCCGATCTGATGCCGTTCTTCTGCTTG)
Primer pair: CXCR4 fragment A: MiSeq_CXCR4_myctag_F (TCTTTCCCTACACGACGCTCTTCCGATCTGCCGAGCAGAAATTGATCAG) and MiSeq_CXCR4_682_R (GTGACTGGAGTTCAGACGTGTGCTCTTCCGATCTAGTGTGACAGCTTGGAGATG)
Primer pair: CXCR4 fragment B: MiSeq_CXCR4_169_F (TCTTTCCCTACACGACGCTCTTCCGATCTGATTGGTCATCCTGGTCATG) and MiSeq_CXCR4_882_R (GTGACTGGAGTTCAGACGTGTGCTCTTCCGATCTACAGTGGAAGAAAGCTAGGG)
Primer pair: CXCR4 fragment C: MiSeq_CXCR4_375_F (TCTTTCCCTACACGACGCTCTTCCGATCTATCCTGGCCTTCATCAGTCT) and MiSeq_Venus_468_R (GTGACTGGAGTTCAGACGTGTGCTCTTCCGATCTGATGCCGTTCTTCTGCTTG)
In a second round of PCR (15 cycles), Illumina adaptors and experiment-specific barcodes were added.
Primer pair for adding I5 and I7 Illumina adaptors: MiSeq_Start_Adaptamer (AATGATACGGCGACCACCGAGATCTACACTCTTTCCCTACACGACGCTCTTCCGATCT) and MiSeq_Index_Adaptamer (CAAGCAGAAGACGGCATACGAGAT-6nt-barcode-GTGACTGGAGTTCAGACGTGTGCTCTTC)
Amplicons were deep sequenced on a HiSeq 2500 using a 2x250 nt paired end protocol, though data is analyzed as single reads.
